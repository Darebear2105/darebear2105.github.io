I'm Looking for a 3D Artist for Mallow Mode

I've been working on an indie game called Mallow Mode, and I've reached the point where I'm realizing something important:
I am not a 3D artist.
I can do 2D art, character art, UI, programming, game design, story, and all that fun stuff, but when it comes to actually making good 3D models...
Yeah. That's not exactly my area of expertise.
Mallow Mode is a 3D RPG with 2D characters, and I'm currently building the game from the ground up. Right now, I'm working with simple placeholder geometry while I figure out the gameplay and world.
The game is still early in development, so this might actually be the first time you've heard of Mallow Mode.
The basic idea is that you play as Mallow, an adventurous alchemist, who discovers a strange purple corruption spreading through the environment. She sets out from her laboratory on a mountain to investigate it, and... well, I'm still figuring out exactly where that adventure is going.
That's part of the fun.

What I'm looking for


Someone who enjoys making stylized 3D models, especially things like:

  • Environment pieces
  • Buildings
  • Rocks and terrain
  • Furniture and props
  • Alchemy equipment
  • Other random things that inevitably become necessary when making a game

You don't have to be some professional 3D artist with 15 years of experience. I'm much more interested in finding someone whose style fits Mallow Mode and who actually wants to work on the project.
I'm also not looking for someone to suddenly make the entire game for me. The project is still being developed, so we'd work on things as they're needed.

What I'm doing

I'm handling the programming, game design, story/worldbuilding, 2D artwork, UI, and pretty much everything else that I can reasonably get my hands on.
You all know I'm also a streamer/YouTuber, and as development gets further along, I plan on sharing the process through my content.
I'm an indie developer, so I don't have a massive budget to throw around. I'm primarily looking for someone who is interested in collaborating on a game rather than treating this like a giant commercial contract. Compensation can be discussed depending on what we're working on and how much work is involved.
If you're interested, you can contact me on Discord (@daoneandonlydareyatoo), or by email (making.mallow.mode@gmail.com). I'd love to see some of your work!
And if you're not a 3D artist yourself but know someone who might be interested, feel free to send them my way.

Even just passing this along could help me find the right person.
